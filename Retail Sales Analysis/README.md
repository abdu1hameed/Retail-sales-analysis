# **Retail Sales Analysis**



### Project Overview



Analysis of retail sales data to identify

sales trends, profitable products, and

business performance.



### Tools Used



1\. SQL

2\. Power BI

3\. Excel

### 

### Key Business Questions Answered



1\. What is the total revenue?

2\. What is the total profit?

3\. Which category generates the highest sales?

4\. Which region generates the highest profit?

5\. What are the top 10 products?

6\. Which month has the highest sales?

7\. Which customers generate the most revenue?

8\. Which products are generating losses?
