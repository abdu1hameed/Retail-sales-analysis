# **5 business observations – Retail Sales Analysis**



### 1\. Technology contributes the highest revenue

### 

&#x20; Observation: Technology generated the highest sales of $836,154.03.



&#x20; Insight: Technology contributes approximately 36.4% of total revenue, making it the strongest revenue-generating category.



&#x20; Recommendation: Focus on maintaining technology inventory and promoting high-performing technology products.



### 2\. West region generates the highest profit

### 

&#x20; Observation: The West region generated the highest profit of $108,418.45.



&#x20; Insight: The West region contributes approximately 37.9% of the company's total profit.



&#x20; Recommendation: Analyze successful sales strategies in the West region and apply them to other regions.



### 3\. Some products are generating significant losses



&#x20; Observation: 301 products have negative overall profit.



&#x20; Insight: The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print generated the highest product-level loss of $8,879.97.



&#x20; Recommendation: Review product pricing, discounts, supplier costs and demand for loss-making products.



### 4\. November has the highest sales



&#x20; Observation: November recorded the highest combined sales across all years, generating $352,461.07.



&#x20; Insight: November contributes approximately 15.3% of total sales, indicating strong demand during this month.



&#x20; Recommendation: Increase inventory and plan promotional campaigns ahead of November.



### 5\. A small group of customers generates significant revenue

### 

&#x20; Observation: Sean Miller is the highest revenue-generating customer, contributing $25,043.05 in sales.



&#x20; Insight: However, this customer generated a negative profit of $1,980.74, despite being the highest revenue contributor.



&#x20; Recommendation: Focus on customer retention while reviewing discounts and pricing to improve customer-level profitability.
